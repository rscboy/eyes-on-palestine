# Research evidence and boundaries

The comparison-page-review.json file records the 100 initial direct page requests, their access results, returned-text sizes, dates and review method. Its URLs are the initially requested routes. The final register sometimes links a more precise chronology route located through primary-site search after an initial request failed. Initial fetch failures are retained and do not establish site downtime or removal.

The research-register.csv/tsv files contain 100 unique final project URLs with paraphrased interface/documentation patterns and inferred lessons. The CSV adds access and hands-on-scope fields. Two Brown Choices entries are explicitly identified as legacy projects whose routes now redirect to the retired program. Multiple projects may belong to the same institution; they are not 100 independent archives.

The interface-observations.md file records the limited browser inspection and interaction performed. No example received a comprehensive keyboard, assistive-technology, mobile or performance audit.

Authoritative standards are linked directly in the report and implementation prompt. Full webpage bodies and full discovery-result dumps are not bundled with these deliverables. Consult the linked original sources for their complete content. The access metadata and paraphrased research register provide the relevant review trail without reproducing entire pages.

The article audit's original titles/summaries come from the user's local archive dataset. The audit is reproducible through build_archive_audit.py and does not assert publisher-body verification or approved event dates.
