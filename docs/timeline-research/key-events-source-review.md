## Access and date limitations

Original report text was consulted where accessible. Some institutional pages and documents blocked direct retrieval; indexed text from the institutional original was consulted in those cases. Each reference records its method. This is not independent verification of every witness account. Related archived articles are further reading, not automatically corroborating evidence for each claim.

“Report published” labels distinguish publication from the underlying period of abuse. The March 2025 reproductive-violence finding retains month precision because institutional release metadata differs between 12 and 13 March; its underlying March 1–31 bounds are for navigation and do not claim a March 1 publication. Nasser’s entry retains OCHA’s 15 February reported-entry date and discloses WHO’s 14 February raid date. The impact section states the reporting date, while its text identifies earlier screening or assessment periods where applicable.

Mass-grave reports preserve uncertainty about identities and circumstances. Detention figures include Gaza and the West Bank. Aid-seeker deaths distinguish distribution sites from convoy routes. Damage assessments distinguish damaged housing from total destruction and preserve assessment dates.
