    Collection, connections and privacy

    Exact duplicate URLs are grouped. The original import remains unchanged. The interactive timeline starts in 2023; earlier records remain in the static reading edition. Draft events are never published automatically.

    Threads are suggestions based on words in imported titles, summaries and categories. Reading routes group sources by subject. Reviewed relationships require supporting passages and editorial approval; they are separate from suggestions.

    This is a text-first timeline. Images come from the imported archive records and load as they enter view. Image context and rights are not independently verified. Hide images at any time; the complete text record remains available.

    Saved citations and notes stay in this browser, without an account. Shared links include your filters and selected source, but never your notes or saved packet. Storage restrictions may prevent persistence.
