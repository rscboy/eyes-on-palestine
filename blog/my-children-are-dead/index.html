My children are dead

A poem by Monique Shanice Wilbert, written September 14, 2025, mourning the children of Gaza.

By Monique Shanice Wilbert · 2026-10-20

On the footsteps to the gate of hell
Far from the garden
Are little bodies wet with blood

Bloated bellied bodies
Behold the gentle face
The purchase of war

Hold them in your hands
Smell the sacred stench of tears
Tears on their breath

Bring them milk and make them drink
Their mouths filled
It is too late

There is no devil here
Only men grinding men into ash
To keep their lands clean

To keep their lands clean
Not dirty with the silt of men
My children are dead

Dead, my politicians. Dead, my sisters and brothers.
Dead, my pastors and laymen of every kind. Dead,
my friends with heavenly compassion on their hearts.
Dead and dying all around us.

-Monique Shanice Wilbert
September 14th, 2025
